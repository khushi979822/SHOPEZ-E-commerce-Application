\# Roles and Responsibilities



\## User



Represents individuals registered on the platform.



Responsibilities:



\* Register and login

\* Browse products

\* Add items to cart

\* Place orders



\## Admin



Responsible for managing the platform.



Responsibilities:



\* Manage categories

\* Manage products

\* Monitor orders



\## Products



Collection of all products available in the platform.



\## Cart



Stores products added to the cart by users.



\## Orders



Stores all completed purchases made by users.
